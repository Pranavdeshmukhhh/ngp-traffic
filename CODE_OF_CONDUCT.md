# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in **NGP Mobility** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior
- Use welcoming, respectful, and constructive language.
- Focus on what is best for the Nagpur civic mobility community.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discriminatory remarks, trolling, or publishing private information (doxxing).

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers for review and removal of offending content or access.
